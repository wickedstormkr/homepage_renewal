Pipeline

Collect and store using global standards, then analyze

Learning data follows global standards from the moment it arrives, and is stored and analyzed just as it was collected.

01 · Collect

Standards-based collection

Real-time collection using global standards

Gathers learning data from all teaching, learning, and operational activities to the same standard.

02 · Store

Storage

Stored with curriculum context

Stores each learning activity with its context, including the curriculum item where it took place.

03 · Analyze

AI analysis

Detects unusual patterns and issues alerts

From accumulated learning data, flags where many learners struggled, in order of priority.

04 · Act

Insight

People review and act

People review the alerts and raw data and make the decision, and the results are recorded as learning data again.

Product · Learning Loop

Three solutions and three roles
complete the Learning Loop

When Lecognizer builds up learning data and finds signals, LearnHubble AI carries those signals into the decisions and actions of administrators, instructors, and learners.

Learning Data Repository

Lecognizer

A learning data repository built on global standards, with AI anomaly detection. Lecognizer collects and stores learning data from all teaching, learning, and operational activities in real time, together with its 1EdTech CASE curriculum context.

  • Real-time collection and storage using global standards, with learning data that carries over when systems change
  • Integrated dashboard and customizable charts, filtered queries and JSON/CSV downloads
  • Used in a national-level learning data hub and in AI platforms for offices of education and enterprises
GS Grade 1 Certification

AI Anomaly Detection

Lecognizer AI

An AI solution that analyzes learning data stored in Lecognizer. It finds unusual patterns and where many learners struggled, flags them, and then shows the raw data behind each alert.

  • Prioritizes signals that need attention and issues alerts
  • Review the conditions behind each alert and the raw learning data in one flow
  • AI finds and analyzes, while administrators and instructors make the decisions

Learning Experience Platform

LearnHubble AI

A personalized teaching and learning platform with AI interaction

In lectures and assignments, AI offers explanations and hints when needed.
